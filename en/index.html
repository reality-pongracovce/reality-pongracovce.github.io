About the property

A space that adapts to your plans

We offer for long-term rent a brick farm building (No. 59) in the village of Pongrácovce, Levoča district, Slovakia. The building has a footprint of 810 m² and an elongated layout with a row of windows along its full length and several separate entrances — the space can easily be divided into smaller operational units.

The building includes office rooms and a water connection with internal distribution. It also comes with a steel shelter of approximately 130 m² for covered parking of machinery or storage — giving you 940 m² of covered space in total. The site has direct access from the local road.

  • Office rooms inside the building
  • Running water installed
  • Divisible premises with separate entrances
  • Steel shelter of ~130 m²
  • Electricity at the property boundary
  • Gas connection at the property boundary
  • No encumbrances in the cadastre
  • Direct from the owner

Location

FAQ

What people ask most often

Didn't find your answer? Write to us — we reply promptly.

What is the rental price?
The price is negotiable — it depends on the extent of the rented premises, optional land and the length of the lease. Tell us about your plans and we will prepare a fair offer.
Can I rent only part of the building?
Yes. The building has an elongated layout with several separate entrances, so it can easily be divided into multiple operational units.
Is land available with the building?
Yes, optionally. A contiguous block of land of approximately 14,560 m² adjacent to the building can be rented — in whole or in part, as needed.
Are utilities available (water, electricity, gas)?
The building has a water connection with internal distribution. A power line and a gas connection point are at the boundary of the property — the tenant may arrange connections according to their needs.
Is there access for trucks?
Yes. The site has direct access from the local road with sufficient manoeuvring space for trucks and agricultural machinery. The D1 motorway (Behárovce exit) is 2 km away.
Is the property legally clear?
Yes, the property is registered in the cadastre free of any encumbrances, and you rent directly from the owner.
